## 1. What was implemented

`ml/training` (new workspace member `intelliai-training`): torch-free
config/manifest layers (pin-verified loading — drift refuses; hash-pure
validation split) + lazy-import LoRA trainer (bf16, grad accumulation +
checkpointing, VRAM measurement, checkpoint round-trip proof) + merge →
CTranslate2 float32 conversion with per-file pins. Heavy deps
(torch cu128 for Blackwell, transformers, peft, soundfile) are an
optional `train` extra — never installed in CI; the cu128 index is
win32-scoped so tts-runtime's Linux cpu-index rule is untouched.
`ml/datasets` gained the Kathbath M4A probe (ffprobe boundary),
deterministic `max_shards` sampling, and multi-source `freeze-train`.

## 11. Model comparison verdict — the ten questions

1. **Did LoRA improve Hindi? NO — it degraded it severely.**
2. Absolute change: **+0.542 CER** (0.3629 → 0.9049).
3. Relative change: **+149%** (worse).
4. Larger than evaluation noise? Yes — ~40× the 0.014 band, replicated.
5. Did WER improve? **No** (0.659 → 1.158).
6. Hallucinations controlled? **No** — 56 probe words vs 0 (engine
   level; the product pipeline's VAD would contain silence but the tone
   probe legitimately passes VAD, so the exposure is real).
7. Did English regress? Essentially no (WER 0.0; one probe word noted).
8. Model size: identical to the incumbent by construction (merged
   244M, CT2 float32 ≈ same footprint; int8 at load).
9. CPU-servable? **No in this state** — RTF 2.9–4.2 breaches the p95 <
   1.5× SLO by an order of magnitude (root cause: constant
   temperature-fallback retries on degenerate decodes).
10. Suitable for the small-model strategy? **This artifact: no.**

## 12. Why it failed [HYPOTHESIS — the 15E-entry questions]

The recipe over-trained: lr 1e-3 (the PEFT-example value) for ~13
epochs on 10 h drove train loss to 0.005 while validation stalled at
0.465. The plausible mechanism is decode-calibration damage causing
repetition/insertion loops. Remedies to test, cheapest first, all
runnable on the same frozen manifests with the machinery now proven:
(a) **evaluate the earlier checkpoints** (500/1000/1500 — already on
disk; ~20 min each); (b) retrain at lr 1e-4–3e-4 for 1–3 epochs
(300–600 steps) with checkpoint selection on validation loss;
(c) constrain the adapter (r=8/16, q-only). None of this ran — 15D
stops here by order.

## 14. Decision recommendation

- **Do not promote; do not proceed to 15E with this artifact.** The
  candidate loses on every axis that matters.
- **The E1 objective — trustworthy evidence — was met.** We now know,
  with committed numbers, that naive-recipe LoRA on 10 h public Hindi
  degrades whisper-small; and we own a proven, reproducible training
  loop that can test the cheap remedies in §12 in hours, not weeks.
- **The Qwen3-ASR engine-adapter track gains priority**: its sandbox
  Hindi reading (CER 0.0796, same clips class) now stands against a
  failed first fine-tune, not just a strong baseline. An adapter
  makes it measurable on the product path — the only route to a legal
  switching test.
